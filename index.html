Our engine

A qanat is the ancient Persian water tunnel: dug by hand, running for miles under the desert, bringing water up at a town. Nobody sees it, and everything grows because of it. That is the job of an engine.

Qanat is the Reformat Games engine. It grew out of Persian Strike, SHAHANSHAH and Galaxy Brain, and every new Reformat game starts from it, so a fix made for one game reaches all of them. Say it ka-NAHT.

17engine modules

The engine decides

Every rule, every random draw and every unit's next step lives in plain C# libraries with no graphics attached, tested headless by their own suites.

Godot draws

The Godot side presents the world the engine runs: cameras, crowds, weather and sound, kept thin so the same simulation can sit under very different games.

Same seed, same game

Simulations are deterministic for a seed and a list of commands, so a battle replays exactly and a bug shows the moment two runs diverge.

About us

The studio

Reformat Games is an independent studio with a soft spot for the machines and genres the industry left behind: cartridge-era strike games, monument builders, card duels, and action movies that play it 90% straight.

We build our games on our own engine, Qanat, so the armies, the weather and the fire learned in one game carry into the next. Our release policy is simple: it ships when it's done.
