How big is your brain?

  1. Small Brain: play a card.
  2. Big Brain: play a card onto your ship, because your ship is the board: a Bridge for your Captain, a fight Deck for your Crew, an Engine Room and Gear slots.
  3. Glowing Brain: let your Captain give Orders, Board the Deck to throw hands, earn Renown, and Ascend into something far less reasonable.
  4. Galaxy Brain: every card you play grows your Brain meter, and at full Galaxy Brain your dumbest, biggest cards come online. It made sense at the time.

Every card is earned by playing. No packs for sale, no premium currency, no "limited offer" pop-ups. Just you, 2,000 cards, and a galaxy that badly needs you to calm down.
