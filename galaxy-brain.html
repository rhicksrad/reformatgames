How big is your brain?

A free online card duel

Galaxy Brain is a deckbuilding card duel set in an original, foul-mouthed space-opera parody. Pick one of three factions, crew your ship with aliens and underpaid robots, and fight other players (or a deeply smug AI) for the hull-cracking Legendaries of the Core Dump set.

  1. Small Brain: play a card.
  2. Big Brain: play a card onto your ship, because your ship is the board: a Bridge for your Captain, a fight Deck for your Crew, an Engine Room and Gear slots.
  3. Glowing Brain: let your Captain give Orders, Board the Deck to throw hands, earn Renown, and Ascend into something far less reasonable.
  4. Galaxy Brain: every card you play grows your Brain meter, and at full Galaxy Brain your dumbest, biggest cards come online. It made sense at the time.

Every card is earned by playing. No packs for sale, no premium currency, no "limited offer" pop-ups. Just you, 2,000 cards, and a galaxy that badly needs you to calm down.

Sanctimonious space monks with glowing energy axes. They don't want to fight you. They want you to think about what you did. Sighs that stop attacks, Auras that shield, and Redactions that blank your best card. Not mad. Just disappointed.

La Costa Mostra Theft

A seafood crime family. Octopus dons, loan sharks in resort shirts, eel hitmen and crabs who will strip your ship for parts. They steal your fuel, lend it back at Vig, and avoid a fair fight at all costs.
